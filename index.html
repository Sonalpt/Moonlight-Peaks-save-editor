🌙 Moonlight Peaks Save Editor

Drop your Moonlight Peaks save here

A JKSV backup .zip, or the loose GameData + GameMetaData files (Switch or PC).

  1. On the Switch, back up Moonlight Peaks with JKSV and copy the backup from sdmc:/JKSV/Moonlight Peaks/ to your PC.
  2. Open it here, edit, then click Download edited save.
  3. Copy the edited zip back into the same JKSV folder and Restore it with the game closed.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Always keep your original backup.
